Health Tips

Drink bottled or purified water. Carry ORS and essential medicines. Use mosquito repellent in monsoon. Wear light cotton clothing. Ensure travel insurance coverage.

Travel Safety

Use ride apps or prepaid taxis. Always wear helmets and seatbelts. Avoid isolated places at night. Check weather alerts during monsoon. Follow beach & backwater safety rules.

Natural Safety

Swim only in lifeguard zones. Beware of strong currents in rivers and backwaters. Avoid trekking during heavy rain. Stay away from cliff edges. Carry flashlight during forest or night trips.

Pharmacies & First Aid

Locate nearest pharmacies using maps. Carry basic first aid kit. Store medicines properly. Keep contact of local doctors & hospitals. Check expiry dates before use.
